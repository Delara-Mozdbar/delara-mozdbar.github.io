EuroSettle Academy

Build 01 — Frontend

EuroSettle Academy

July – August 2026

  1. 01 Frontend
  2. 02 Full-stack
  3. 03 Full-stack + Deploy

An online video academy for newcomers to Europe — language and practical life skills, organized by destination country.

  • 12 topics grouped into three relocation stages: First Steps, Work & Career, Daily Life
  • Country browser, video detail pages, and full-text search with country/topic/subtitle filters
  • Phase one: frontend only with mock data, to validate the idea and information architecture

Orbitask

Build 02 — Full-stack

Orbitask

July – August 2026

  1. 01 Frontend
  2. 02 Full-stack
  3. 03 Full-stack + Deploy

A team task and project management platform — Asana-style workspaces combined with Trello-style Kanban boards.

  • Workspaces, projects, and Kanban boards (To Do / In Progress / Done)
  • Two-level role-based access (workspace and project roles)
  • Notifications on task assignment, plus calendar, timeline, and dashboard views
  • Backend built first with Next.js Route Handlers and tested in Postman

Ticketa

Build 03 — Full-stack + Deploy

Ticketa

July – August 2026

  1. 01 Frontend
  2. 02 Full-stack
  3. 03 Full-stack + Deploy

An event and ticketing marketplace — discover events, get tickets, or host your own.

  • Passwordless login with one-time codes (OTP)
  • Three roles with dedicated dashboards: organizer, attendee, admin
  • Automated reminders one hour before events via Vercel Cron
  • Demo mode: OTP shown on screen, payments simulated
